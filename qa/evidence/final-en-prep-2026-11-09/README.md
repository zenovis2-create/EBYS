Raw TAP outputs: `validator-selftests.tap`, `gap12-synthetic.tap`. The validator self-tests use explicitly synthetic in-memory receipt/media/model metadata and must never be counted as actual raw capture or live-model evidence. Final owner readiness is tracked in `../../FINAL-EN-READINESS.md`; pending template is `../../final-en-receipt.template.json`.
